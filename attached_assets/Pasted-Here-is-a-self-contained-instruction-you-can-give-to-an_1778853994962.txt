## High‑level requirement

On the new GEM website, implement a **“Partners & Trustees” section** that shows a **horizontally moving strip of partner/trustee logos and labels**, scrolling smoothly from right to left in an infinite loop.  
The component must be driven by GEM’s **project description and service lines** (cybersecurity, digital threat monitoring, asset recovery, real estate via Alliance Trust Realty, trust/legal services, investment, quantum financial system, etc.), not by a fixed list of specific brands.[1]

***

## What to build

1. Section structure  
   - Add a section on the homepage with:  
     - Title: “Our Trusted Partners & Business Allies”  
     - Subtitle: “Working with world-class organizations to deliver excellence.”[1]
   - Place it near the top of the page, close to the hero and core services, so it clearly signals credibility.[1]

2. Component  
   - Implement a reusable component, for example `PartnersTrusteesCarousel`.  
   - This component should render a **single horizontal row** of cards. Each card represents a partner or trustee (or a partner “slot” if names are not final yet).

3. Visual pattern and motion  
   - Layout:  
     - Cards aligned in one horizontal line inside a container with `overflow: hidden`.  
     - Each card shows a logo (or placeholder icon), a name, and a short role label, such as:  
       - “Tier‑1 Cybersecurity Partner”  
       - “Asset Recovery Trustee”  
       - “Real Estate Brokerage Alliance”  
       - “Legal & Trust Services Partner”  
       - “Quantum Financial Infrastructure Partner”  
   - Motion:  
     - The row scrolls **continuously from right to left** (anti‑clockwise) in a smooth, marquee‑style animation.  
     - The animation loops infinitely, with no visual “jump” at the end.  
     - On desktop: pause the animation on hover if possible.  
     - On mobile: keep it moving continuously (no hover).

4. Data‑driven design (no hard‑coded big tech names)  
   - Do **not** assume partners must be Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Cisco, etc.  
   - Instead, the carousel must be powered by a **data structure inferred from GEM’s project and services** as listed on the current site:  
     - Cybersecurity  
     - Threat monitoring  
     - Asset recovery  
     - Real estate services via Alliance Trust Realty  
     - Legal documentation and power of attorney  
     - Investment portfolio  
     - Quantum financial system  
     - Market insights and related advisory services[1]
   - From these, define partner/trustee items like:  
     - `name`: either the actual organization name (if known later) or a role‑based placeholder (e.g., “Global Threat Intelligence Partner”).  
     - `segment`: category such as “Cybersecurity”, “Real Estate”, “Trust & Legal”, “Financial & QFS”.  
     - `role`: one short phrase tying them to our offering.  
     - `logo`: image path (can start as a neutral placeholder icon per segment).  
     - `link`: optional URL to either an internal “Partner Details” page or an external official website.  
   - The agent should design this so that when GEM later confirms real organizations, you only update this data array; the layout and animation stay the same.

5. Technical behavior and responsiveness  
   - Continuous animation using either:  
     - A small, dependency‑light marquee/slider library that supports infinite horizontal looping, or  
     - Custom CSS keyframes and a duplicated track (list of cards repeated twice) to get a seamless loop.  
   - Make speed configurable (e.g., 20–40 seconds per full loop) and test for legibility.  
   - Ensure responsive design:  
     - On wide screens, multiple cards visible at once.  
     - On small screens, cards remain readable and the strip still scrolls horizontally inside the viewport.  
   - The component must be reusable on the dedicated “Partners & Trustees” page, which already exists in the navigation.[1]
